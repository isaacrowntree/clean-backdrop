How It Works

Technique 1

Shadow Lift

Blends cast shadows toward the sampled clean wall color. No AI pixel replacement — just smart color correction that preserves the natural wall gradient. Adjustable strength from 0–100%.

Technique 2

Marks & Blemishes

LaMa AI inpainting targets only small isolated marks — wrinkles, scuffs, seams, dust. Capped at 5% of the image to prevent smudging. Your 4070 Ti processes it in seconds.

Technique 3

Subject Protection

rembg segmentation ensures the subject is never modified. Sharp edges with minimal feathering — no ghosting, no color bleed. ICC profiles and EXIF metadata preserved.

# Install dependencies
